# Upgrading, downgrading and seats

Tasklane has four plans: Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise.

- Upgrades take effect immediately. The price difference for the rest of the billing period is prorated and charged straight away.
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Until then the workspace keeps all features of the current plan.
- Seats can be added at any time in Settings > Billing > Seats. New seats are prorated for the rest of the billing period.
- Removed seats stay active until the period ends and are credited at the next renewal.

Moving from Free to a paid plan keeps all existing boards and members. Moving to a plan with fewer features, for example from Business to Pro, turns off features such as SAML single sign-on at the end of the period.
