# Refund policy

Refunds depend on the plan type and the reason for the request.

- Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Cancelling a monthly plan stops the next renewal, and the workspace keeps full access until the end of the paid month.
- Annual plans can be refunded pro-rata for unused full months if the request is made within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date.
- Duplicate charges are always refunded in full. The money returns to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

To request a refund, open Settings > Billing > Invoices, pick the invoice and choose Request refund. You can also reply to any billing email from support with the invoice number and the reason. Refund requests are reviewed by the billing team, who confirm the amount before it is sent back.
