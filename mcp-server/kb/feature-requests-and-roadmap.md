# Feature requests and the roadmap

Feature requests are collected on the public feedback board at https://feedback.tasklane.example. Anyone with a Tasklane account can post a request and vote on existing ones. Before posting, search the board, because many ideas already have a thread.

The product team reviews the board every month and updates the status of the most voted items. Support cannot promise delivery dates for any feature, because plans change as the team learns more.

A timeline view is listed on the board with the status "under consideration". Voting for it and adding a short description of your use case helps the team decide.

Enterprise customers can also ask for a call with the product team to discuss their needs in more detail.
