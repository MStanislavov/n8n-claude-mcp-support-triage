# Updating your payment method

Tasklane accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Business and Enterprise plans can also pay by SEPA direct debit.

To change the payment method, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and add the new card or mandate. The new method is used from the next renewal. Only workspace owners and billing admins can see this page.

If a payment fails, Tasklane retries it 3 times over 7 days and emails the billing contact after each attempt. If all retries fail, the workspace becomes read-only until a payment succeeds. Nothing is deleted while a workspace is read-only, and full access returns as soon as the payment goes through.

Update the payment method before the retry window ends to avoid the read-only state.
