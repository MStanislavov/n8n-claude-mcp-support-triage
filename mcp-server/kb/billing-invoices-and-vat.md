# Invoices and VAT IDs

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact after every charge. Every invoice can also be downloaded as a PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices, which lists all invoices for the workspace.

To add or change a VAT ID, enter it in Settings > Billing > Company details. A VAT ID applies to future invoices only. Invoices that were already issued are not changed automatically.

Invoices from the last 90 days can be reissued with a corrected company name, address or VAT ID. Send the invoice number and the corrected details to support and the billing team will issue a replacement. Older invoices cannot be reissued, but a copy can still be downloaded.

The billing contact can be changed by any workspace owner in Settings > Billing > Billing contact.
