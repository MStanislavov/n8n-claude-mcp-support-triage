# Password reset and two-factor authentication

To reset a password, choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes and is sent only to the email address on the account. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder and make sure the address is the one used to sign up. People often have accounts under an older work or personal address.

If you lose the device used for two-factor authentication, use one of your recovery codes to sign in and set up a new device. Recovery codes are shown once when two-factor authentication is turned on.

Without recovery codes, the workspace owner must confirm your identity, which takes 1 to 2 business days. For security, support cannot tell someone which email address an account uses unless they can verify that they own it.
