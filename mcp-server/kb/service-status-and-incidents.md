# Service status and incident response

The live status of Tasklane is shown at https://status.tasklane.example. Incidents are posted there within 15 minutes of detection and updated every 30 minutes until they are resolved.

Business and Enterprise plans include a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. When uptime falls below the target, the service credit is 10% of the monthly fee for every 0.1% below target, capped at 50%. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident by contacting support.

Outage reports from Business and Enterprise customers are paged to the on-call engineer 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. To help the engineer, include the workspace URL, the time the problem was first seen and the exact error message or a screenshot. Free and Pro customers are handled during business hours and can follow the status page for updates.
